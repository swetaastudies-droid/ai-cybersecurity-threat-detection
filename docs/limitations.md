# Limitations

- UNSW-NB15 is a research benchmark and may not represent current enterprise traffic.
- Model scores are not calibrated estimates of real-world compromise probability.
- False positives and false negatives require analysis before operational use.
- Isolation Forest anomaly scores are distinct from classifier attack probabilities.
- The dashboard visualizes saved benchmark predictions, not live telemetry.
- Do not upload confidential logs, credentials, or personal data.
